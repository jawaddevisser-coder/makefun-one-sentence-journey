Entry for the [One Sentence, One Journey](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gudril-cool-one-sentence-one-journey-410686ca) bounty on make.fun.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes a single complete story about a journey — told in exactly one sentence — to `/output/entry.txt`.

The story: a woman leaves Lisbon at dawn with her grandfather's brass compass and an unreadable ticket, crosses borders on trains smelling of diesel and boiled potatoes, and discovers at the last stop that the compass had been pointing home all along.
